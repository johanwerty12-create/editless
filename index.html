FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No signup, no watermark, no export limits.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens inside your browser on your own device.

How long does export take?

About as long as the finished video, because it is rendered in real time.

What does it work best on?

Videos where someone is talking. Music-only or very noisy videos may not cut well, so check the green and red timeline before exporting.

Which browsers work?

Recent Chrome, Edge and Safari. If export fails, try Chrome.
